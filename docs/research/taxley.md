# Taxley — salary vs dividends calculator
URL: https://taxley.co.uk/salary-vs-dividends-calculator

## What it does
- Profit input, 2025/26 and 2026/27, salary optimiser "to the penny".
- Other income, Scotland, associated companies, Employment Allowance.
- Every-option table, worked example, detailed assumptions, sources, last reviewed.
- Free MCP server and embeddable iframe; CT600 filing upsell.

## Strengths
Best-in-class transparency and freshness. Explains the 2026 dividend rise and
why the old rule broke. Strong internal linking to related calculators.

## Weaknesses
- One tax year at a time.
- Pension lives in a separate calculator, not the optimiser.
- No shareable result URL.
- No multi-year extraction view.
- Lead-gen toward filing software.
