# Frazer James — salary vs dividend calculator
URL: https://frazerjames.co.uk/salary-vs-dividend-calculator/
Fetched: 2026-11-11

## What it does
- Input the total you plan to take out and how much as salary.
- Shows your split vs the best split, corporation tax rate selector, workings.
- Adviser-reviewed, FCA-regulated lead-gen for financial planning.

## Strengths
Clear "same total out, like-for-like" framing. Good explanation of the April
2026 change and the pension alternative.

## Weaknesses
- No pension in the model.
- No year comparison (2025/26 absent).
- Single year, no retained-profit view.
- Lead-gen; no shareable results.
