# Eternity Accountants — salary and dividend calculator
URL: https://eternityaccountants.co.uk/calculators/salary-and-dividend-calculator/
Fetched: 2026-11-11

## What it does
- Two directors, share split, salary auto/manual, retained profit, other income,
  employer pension, student loan plans, Scotland, Child Benefit.
- Charts, PDF/print, tax diary, payments on account, dividend paperwork guidance.

## Strengths
Widest input set among the reviewed tools. Strong practical guidance (January
payments on account, dividend minutes, director's loan).

## Weaknesses
- Extremely long page (15 sections) with competing CTAs.
- One-year projection only.
- Lead-gen for accounting services.
- Their published £150,000 row (best salary £5,000, take-home £85,320.94) does
  not reconcile with our engine or with their own £100,000 row; we match them at
  £30k/£50k/£60k/£100k. Treat the £150k row as unverified.
