# QA — DrawDesk v1

**Verdict: RELEASE.** Zero blockers. Three non-blocking findings recorded.

## Scope
Live URL https://drawdesk.76-13-181-161.sslip.io/ (own server) plus the local production export and the Pages mirror.
Chromium 1200 (Playwright) at 1440×900 and 390×844. Build from `main` at the
release commit. Date 2026-11-11.

## Blocking findings
None.

## Findings (non-blocking)
- **QA-1 [minor] Initial JS ~180 KB gzip** against a 150 KB house budget. LCP is
  still 1.1 s and Lighthouse performance 93. Follow-up: island/lighter shell in
  v1.1. Not hidden.
- **QA-2 [minor] No custom domain.** Canonical and OG use the Pages URL. Owner
  action: register `drawdesk.co.uk`, then switch the two build variables and DNS.
- **QA-3 [note] £150,000 reference divergence.** Our engine gives £87,237; one
  competitor publishes £85,320.94 for the same row while matching us exactly at
  £30k/£50k/£60k/£100k. Our model is internally reconciled (take-home + tax =
  profit) and tested; the difference is documented in `docs/01-market.md` and
  `docs/research/eternity.md`.
