# Brand — director pay planning

## Territories
1. **Draw / drawings** — the UK language of taking money out of a company
   ("drawings", "draw a salary"). Owns the action, not the tax jargon.
2. **Desk / plan** — a working tool, not a lead-gen page. Signals planning over
   a one-off calculation.
3. **Split / mix** — the actual decision (salary vs dividend vs pension), but
   "split" names are crowded and generic.

Winner territory: **Draw + Desk** — action + tool, short, human, and adjacent to
the query language without being a keyword-stuffed domain.

## The lock
**Name:** DrawDesk
**Say:** DRAW-desk
**Means:** the desk where a director decides what to draw.
**Beat:** DrawWise (softer, sounds like advice we are not allowed to give) and
DiviDesk (clearer niche but "divi" excludes salary and pension).

## Verbal kit
- **Tagline:** Plan what you draw.
- **One-liner:** DrawDesk finds the salary, dividend and pension mix that leaves
  a UK director with the most, this year and the next five.
- **50 words:** DrawDesk is a free planner for UK limited-company directors. It
  finds the best salary and dividend mix for your profit, adds employer pension
  and retained profit, and shows the next one to five years — with every rate
  sourced, the workings visible, and no signup.
- **Meta title:** Director Salary vs Dividend Planner 2026/27 — DrawDesk
- **Meta description:** Plan your director pay: best salary, dividend and
  pension mix for 2025/26 and 2026/27, plus a 5-year view. Free, no signup.
- **Tone:** plain, numeric, calm. Short sentences. Admit assumptions.
- **Avoid:** "maximise your wealth", "tax loophole", "HMRC hates this",
  "guaranteed", "advice".
